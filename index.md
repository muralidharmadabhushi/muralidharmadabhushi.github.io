### Bio

I am an Assistant Research Professor at the Wyant College of Optical Sciences, University of Arizona. I lead the coherent imaging efforts in the [Computational 3D Imaging and Measurement Lab](https://3dim.optics.arizona.edu/) and hold leadership roles on several projects funded by federal and industry partners. My research lies at the intersection of computational imaging and coherent imaging, with a particular focus on imaging through disorder. My expertise is in the design and development of advanced coherent imaging and laser-based optical systems for applications like non-line-of-sight imaging, imaging through tissue, and free-space optical communications. 

My dissertation work integrated tunable diode lasers, neuromorphic focal-plane arrays, and computational processing techniques to enable imaging through heavily scattering media. My research has also advanced the use of neuromorphic cameras for computational imaging, including the first demonstration of combining laser speckle with neuromorphic cameras to probe densely scattering media like tissue.

I received my Bachelor’s degree in Electronics and Instrumentation from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University in 2014, and a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Southern Methodist University in 2024.
